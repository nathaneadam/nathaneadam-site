Video basics

Interactive lessons on making video: the camera, framing, light, sound, editing and story. In each one you guess first, then you get to play with it, then a short quiz checks that it stuck.

About 20 minutes a lessonFirst lesson live now

Twenty minutes is on purpose. It’s the first step of how I think about learning anything: spend 20 minutes on a topic. If it grabs you, give it 20 hours. If you still love it, 200. Very few things earn 2,000. This course is a stack of 20-minute starts, so you can find out which one is yours.

I’m building it one lesson at a time. Lessons that aren’t built yet say so.
